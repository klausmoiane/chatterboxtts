On the night of Monday, November 2, just hours before polls opened in the United States, Donald Trump was asked whether he would bomb Iran again after the elections, He answered with a single word, no, Shortly afterward, American fighter jets attacked the Iranian island of Qeshm, in the Strait of Hormuz, And it is this contrast between campaign rhetoric and the reality in the Gulf that sets the stage for the latest developments over the past 24 hours

According to American officials speaking to CBS and Axios, the Iranian Revolutionary Guard Corps fired a surface-to-air missile at a U S aircraft over the strait, and an American fighter jet destroyed the launcher on Qeshm in an operation described as limited and defensive, rather than a resumption of the war, The Wall Street Journal went further, reporting that the strike was part of a series of limited attacks Washington has been carrying out quietly in recent weeks, aimed at stripping Iran of its ability to threaten commercial shipping

On the diplomatic front, the day was marked by cautious optimism, Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baghaei said the ball was in America's court and that the Strait of Hormuz would only reopen once the naval blockade of Iranian ports ended, frozen assets were released, and sanctions on Iranian oil were eased, He presented the reduction of the proposed plan from seven days to two as proof of goodwill

Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi traveled to Doha to help draft a preliminary agreement, while Axios reported that Vice President JD Vance had sent a letter to Iran's Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council, Mohsen Rezaei, marking the highest-level contact between the two sides in months

Vance said that he and Trump were quite confident about reaching an agreement, while the president himself wrote that Iran wanted a deal as soon as possible, However, in an interview with Axios, Trump left the door open, saying that he would do something one way or another, but that there was no rush

What makes the situation even more interesting is the division within the administration, According to Axios, CIA Director John Ratcliffe, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, and Secretary of State Marco Rubio favor maintaining economic pressure without going to war or reaching an agreement, while Vance and special envoy Steve Witkoff are pushing Trump toward the negotiating table

On the other side, the Associated Press reported that Benjamin Netanyahu is working with members of the administration to convince the president to launch a rapid and extensive air campaign, with Arab allies warning that such an operation could draw the Houthis and Iraqi militias directly into the conflict

The markets chose to bet on diplomacy, with Brent crude falling more than 7% and closing near $105 a barrel

However, the fighting did not stop, The British maritime security agency UKMTO reported that a vessel was struck by an unidentified projectile approximately 10 nautical miles off the coast of Limassol, causing damage to its hull, All crew members were reported safe

Meanwhile, Iranian media reported that another oil tanker caught fire after striking a mine at the eastern entrance to the strait, It is worth remembering that UKMTO itself recorded 30 attacks on commercial vessels in October alone, a record high

On the same day, Rezaei spoke by telephone with Vladimir Putin and thanked him for Russia's support, serving as a reminder that Moscow remains involved in the geopolitical equation

The regional crisis is also escalating in Yemen, According to Reuters, the Houthis have advanced to within approximately 80 kilometers of Aden, the provisional capital of the internationally recognized government, after capturing the Al-Maqatirah district, This represents their deepest advance toward the city since 2015

The rebels also claimed to have launched drone attacks against Dammam Airport in Saudi Arabia, while the Saudi-led coalition said it had intercepted two drones in the region of the capital

In Israel, the electoral battle has become more clearly defined, Netanyahu stated that the majority of the Jewish public had chosen Likud and had chosen him to lead the next government, He advocated for a broad coalition without Arab parties, which secured a record 19 seats

The opposition bloc that has rallied behind Gadi Eisenkot scheduled its first meeting for Tuesday, but remains divided precisely over the question of Arab parties, with Yair Golan supporting their inclusion and Avigdor Lieberman firmly opposed

President Isaac Herzog is scheduled to begin consultations on Wednesday

In Ukraine, during his Monday evening address, Volodymyr Zelensky said intelligence indicated that Russia was preparing another massive attack against energy and logistics infrastructure, betting that the world's attention would be focused on the American elections and the shortage of Patriot missiles

Hours later, during the early hours of Tuesday morning, the attack came

Ukrainian monitoring groups reported approximately 20 ballistic missiles and 50 cruise missiles, including, according to these channels, Zircon hypersonic missiles targeting Ladyzhyn, home to a major thermal power plant, and North Korean KN-23 missiles targeting Zelenodolsk, near another power facility, However, the Ukrainian Air Force had not yet released its official assessment

In Kyiv, an attack struck a hospital in the Mamytslensky district, leaving 62 people injured and forcing the transfer of 226 patients, according to regional governor Oleh Syniehubov

In Dnipro, strikes hit critical infrastructure and residential buildings, leaving five people injured, This prompted Ukrenergo to introduce emergency power cuts across parts of the Kyiv and Dnipropetrovsk regions

Ukraine's response came with considerable force

According to Moscow Mayor Sergei Sobyanin, 493 drones flew toward the Moscow region between Monday night and Tuesday morning, with 187 reportedly intercepted near the capital, It was one of the largest attacks targeting the city in six months, with Vnukovo and Domodedovo airports closed for part of the night

Zelensky had summarized this strategy by saying that as long as the war continues, Russian oil will burn

Poland's institutional crisis is heading toward a decisive test this Tuesday, Prime Minister Donald Tusk said the solution was simple and did not require changing the Constitution, all that was needed was for President Karol Nawrocki to recognize the judges elected by parliament, He accused the president of choosing a power struggle that could prove costly for the country

The Constitutional Tribunal, which has been surrounded by police officers and protesters since Friday, is scheduled to hold an assembly of judges today, convened by Judge Anna Korwin-Piotrowska

However, the assembly can only make decisions with 10 of the 15 judges present, requiring the support of at least two judges appointed during the PiS government

The European Commission reiterated that the appointment of new judges falls under the authority of the Polish Parliament

In the Indo-Pacific, Taiwan recorded 19 sorties by Chinese military aircraft and seven Chinese naval vessels operating around the island

Meanwhile, the South China Morning Post, citing Japanese intelligence, reported that China is building or equipping six Type 071 amphibious assault ships at two shipyards, These are precisely the kinds of vessels considered essential for a large-scale invasion operation

The Japan Times reported that the government of Sanae Takaichi is considering spending between 70 and 80 trillion yen on defense over the five years beginning in 2027, nearly double the current plan

In Colombia, the day was marked by mourning and an investigation after a truck loaded with explosives detonated near the military base in Planadas, in southern Tolima, on Sunday night

The explosion killed four civilians, including a two-month-old baby, and damaged homes and the town's hospital

Colombian media have identified the Jerónimo Galeano structure, part of the FARC dissident groups commanded by Iván Mordisco, as a possible suspect, However, authorities are still investigating responsibility for the attack
